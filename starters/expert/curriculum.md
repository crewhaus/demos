# Curriculum — specialty coffee brewing & extraction science

This is the expert's **learning plan**: what to learn, in a defensible
order, and where the trustworthy sources are. `/study` reads this to decide
what to learn next when there are no higher-priority knowledge gaps.
`/reflect` **edits** it — ticking mastered rungs and adding rungs for gaps
the expert keeps hitting.

> Priority order for any study pass:
> 1. Open **knowledge gaps** logged via `log_knowledge_gap` (the expert's
>    own measured blind spots) — always first.
> 2. The next unchecked rung on the **ladder** below (formal curriculum).
> 3. **What's new** — recent high-quality developments (see § Frontier).

A rung is "mastered" when there's a `verified: true` wiki article that
covers it AND the exam has a passing question for it. Tick it then — not
before.

## Ladder (learn top-to-bottom)

### Tier 1 — Fundamentals (time-tested; learn first)
- [ ] Extraction basics: solubles, extraction yield (EY %), TDS, and the
      brew-ratio/strength/extraction relationship
- [ ] The SCA "Golden Cup" control chart: target EY 18–22%, TDS 1.15–1.35%
- [ ] Grind size → surface area → extraction rate; grind distribution
- [ ] Water: temperature, TDS/hardness, buffer (SCA/ideal water spec)
- [ ] Time & agitation as extraction levers
- [ ] Roast degree and its effect on solubility and extraction

### Tier 2 — Methods & mechanics
- [ ] Percolation vs immersion (pour-over vs French press/steep) — how each
      maps onto the extraction levers
- [ ] Espresso: dose, yield, ratio, pressure, puck resistance, channelling
- [ ] Filter geometry & flow (V60 vs flat-bottom vs Kalita)
- [ ] Recipe design: dialing in by taste (sour = under, bitter = over)

### Tier 3 — Measurement & sensory
- [ ] Refractometry: measuring TDS, computing EY, sampling error
- [ ] SCA cupping protocol and the sensory lexicon / flavour wheel
- [ ] Green grading & defect counting basics

### Frontier — recent / provisional (mark confidence honestly)
- [ ] Recent extraction-uniformity research (e.g. grind bimodality, fines)
- [ ] Pressure-profiling and flow-profiling espresso findings
- [ ] Any new standards revisions from SCA / CoffeeMind / peer-reviewed work

## Source allowlist (high quality → weight these)

**Primary / standards (highest trust):**
- Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) standards & protocols — sca.coffee
- Peer-reviewed food science (e.g. *Matter*, *Scientific Reports*,
  *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry*) on coffee extraction
- "The Physics of Filter Coffee" (Hendon et al.) and related academic work

**Canonical practitioner texts (high trust):**
- Barista Hustle (barista-focused courses/reference)
- CoffeeMind / Colonna-Dashwood writing on extraction & water

**Use with care (corroborate before committing):**
- Reputable industry blogs and equipment makers' technical notes
- Forums (only for leads to verify against a primary source)

Rule: a claim gets committed to the wiki only with a source. Prefer a
primary or peer-reviewed source; if only a blog supports it, set a lower
`confidenceScore` and flag it for re-verification in `/reflect`.

## Retargeting to another field

This file is domain-specific on purpose. To make the expert an expert in a
different field, replace the ladder with that field's real learning path
(a degree syllabus, a professional certification outline, a canonical
textbook's table of contents) and the allowlist with that field's primary
sources. The **mechanism** — gaps-first, then the ladder, then the frontier
— is the same for any field.
